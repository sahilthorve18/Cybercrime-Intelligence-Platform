Indian IT Act 2000 & Cyber Laws

Section 43

Unauthorized Access & Data Damage

Penalty up to ₹1 Crore for hacking, data theft, virus introduction, or denial of service attacks.

Section 66

Computer Related Offences

Imprisonment up to 3 years and fine up to ₹5 Lakhs for hacking with malicious intent.

Section 66C

Identity Theft

Imprisonment up to 3 years and fine up to ₹1 Lakh for fraudulent use of another person's identity.

Section 66D

Cheating by Impersonation

Imprisonment up to 3 years and fine up to ₹1 Lakh for online fraud using fake identity.

Section 66E

Privacy Violation

Imprisonment up to 3 years or fine up to ₹2 Lakhs for capturing/publishing private images without consent.

Section 66F

Cyber Terrorism

Life imprisonment for acts threatening national security, public safety, or critical infrastructure.

Section 67

Publishing Obscene Content

Imprisonment up to 3 years and fine up to ₹5 Lakhs for first conviction; 5 years for subsequent.

Section 72

Breach of Confidentiality

Imprisonment up to 2 years and fine up to ₹1 Lakh for unauthorized disclosure of personal information.

📜 Detailed Legal Provisions

Section 43 - Penalty for Damage to Computer Systems

Applicable When:

  • Unauthorized access to computer systems or networks
  • Downloading, copying, or extracting data without permission
  • Introducing viruses or malicious code
  • Damaging or deleting data
  • Disrupting or denying access to computer resources

Penalty: Compensation up to ₹1 Crore to affected party

Real Example: Company employee deletes customer database before resignation - liable under Section 43

Section 66 - Hacking with Criminal Intent

Covers: All acts under Section 43 done with malicious or fraudulent intent

Penalty: Imprisonment up to 3 years OR fine up to ₹5 Lakhs OR both

Key Difference from 43: Section 43 is civil (compensation), Section 66 is criminal (imprisonment)

Section 66C - Identity Theft

Covers: Fraudulent use of electronic signature, password, or unique identification of another person

Common Scenarios:

  • Using someone else's credit card details for online purchases
  • Creating fake social media profiles impersonating real people
  • Using stolen login credentials to access bank accounts
  • Email spoofing to impersonate company executives

Penalty: Imprisonment up to 3 years AND fine up to ₹1 Lakh

Section 66D - Cheating by Personation Using Computer

Covers: Cheating by impersonation using computer resources

Examples:

  • Phishing emails pretending to be from banks
  • Fake customer support calls claiming to be from tech companies
  • Romance scams using fake dating profiles
  • Job scams impersonating legitimate companies

Penalty: Imprisonment up to 3 years AND fine up to ₹1 Lakh

Section 67 - Publishing Obscene Material

Covers: Publishing or transmitting obscene material in electronic form

Penalty:

  • First Conviction: Imprisonment up to 3 years AND fine up to ₹5 Lakhs
  • Subsequent Conviction: Imprisonment up to 5 years AND fine up to ₹10 Lakhs

Note: Section 67A covers sexually explicit content, 67B covers child pornography (stricter penalties)

⏱️ Golden Hour Rule

Report cybercrime within the first hour for maximum fund recovery chances. The National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal can freeze fraudulent transactions if reported immediately.

  • Within 1 hour: 70% fund recovery rate
  • Within 24 hours: 40% fund recovery rate
  • After 48 hours: Less than 10% recovery rate
