## 2. Attack Vectors & Techniques

### 2.1 Social Engineering Attacks

#### Vishing (Voice Phishing)
**Prevalence:** 40% of all social engineering attacks

**Common Scripts:**
1. **Bank Fraud Alert:**
   - "Your account has been compromised"
   - "Verify your details to prevent blocking"
   - "Share OTP to secure your account"

2. **KYC Update Scam:**
   - "Your KYC is incomplete"
   - "Account will be suspended in 24 hours"
   - "Update details via link/call"

3. **Prize/Lottery Scam:**
   - "You've won ₹25 lakhs"
   - "Pay processing fee to claim"
   - "Limited time offer"

**Success Rate:** 5-8% of calls result in victim engagement

#### Phishing Emails
**Prevalence:** 35% of attacks

**Common Themes:**
- Fake delivery notifications (Amazon, Flipkart)
- Tax refund alerts (Income Tax Department)
- Job offers requiring upfront payment
- Investment opportunities
- Account verification requests

**Click-Through Rate:** 12-15%  
**Credential Submission Rate:** 3-5%

#### SMS Phishing (Smishing)
**Prevalence:** 25% of attacks

**Common Messages:**
- "Your parcel is pending delivery - click link"
- "Your account will be blocked - verify now"
- "You have received ₹X - claim here"
- "Electricity bill overdue - pay now"

**Click Rate:** 8-10%

## 7. Defensive Strategies & Countermeasures

### 7.1 Individual Protection

#### Essential Security Measures
**Priority Level: CRITICAL**

1. **Multi-Factor Authentication (2FA)**
   - Effectiveness: 98% reduction in account takeover
   - Recommended: App-based (Google Authenticator, Authy)
   - Avoid: SMS-based (vulnerable to SIM swap)

2. **Password Management**
   - Use unique passwords for each account
   - Minimum 12 characters (mixed case, numbers, symbols)
   - Password manager recommended (Bitwarden, 1Password, LastPass)
   - Change passwords every 90 days

3. **Software Updates**
   - Enable automatic updates
   - Update within 48 hours of release
   - Covers 85% of known vulnerabilities

4. **Antivirus/Anti-malware**
   - Real-time protection enabled
   - Weekly full system scans
   - Recommended: Windows Defender, Kaspersky, Bitdefender

5. **Backup Strategy (3-2-1 Rule)**
   - 3 copies of data
   - 2 different storage types
   - 1 offsite/offline backup
   - Recovery success rate: 95%

#### Behavioral Security
**Priority Level: HIGH**

1. **Email Vigilance**
   - Verify sender email addresses
   - Don't click suspicious links
   - Hover over links to preview URL
   - Check for spelling/grammar errors

2. **Phone Call Verification**
   - Never share OTP/passwords over phone
   - Verify caller identity independently
   - Use video calls for emergency verification
   - Hang up and call back on official numbers

3. **Social Media Privacy**
   - Limit personal information sharing
   - Review privacy settings quarterly
   - Be cautious with voice/video posts
   - Verify friend requests

4. **Financial Transactions**
   - Enable transaction alerts
   - Set daily transaction limits
   - Use virtual cards for online shopping
   - Monitor statements weekly

### 7.2 Organizational Protection

#### Technical Controls
**Priority Level: CRITICAL**

1. **Network Security**
   - Firewall configuration
   - Intrusion Detection/Prevention Systems (IDS/IPS)
   - Network segmentation
   - VPN for remote access

2. **Endpoint Protection**
   - Enterprise antivirus/EDR
   - Device encryption
   - Mobile Device Management (MDM)
   - Application whitelisting

3. **Access Control**
   - Principle of least privilege
   - Role-based access control (RBAC)
   - Regular access reviews
   - Privileged access management (PAM)

4. **Data Protection**
   - Encryption at rest and in transit
   - Data Loss Prevention (DLP)
   - Regular backups (automated)
   - Disaster recovery plan

#### Organizational Measures
**Priority Level: HIGH**

1. **Security Awareness Training**
   - Quarterly training sessions
   - Phishing simulation exercises
   - Incident reporting procedures
   - Security champions program

2. **Incident Response Plan**
   - Defined roles and responsibilities
   - Communication protocols
   - Escalation procedures
   - Regular drills (quarterly)

3. **Vendor Risk Management**
   - Security assessments
   - Contractual security requirements
   - Regular audits
   - Incident notification clauses

4. **Compliance & Audits**
   - Regular security audits
   - Penetration testing (annual)
   - Compliance monitoring (GDPR, DPDP Act)
   - Vulnerability assessments
