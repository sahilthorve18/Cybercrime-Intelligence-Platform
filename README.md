# 🛡️ CyberInsight Elite - Cybercrime Intelligence Platform

An advanced analytical platform that studies cybercrime patterns, awareness levels, and behavioral risk modeling to improve digital safety across India.

## 📊 Platform Overview

CyberInsight Elite is a comprehensive web-based intelligence platform designed to analyze cybercrime trends, educate users about cyber threats, and provide actionable security recommendations based on real-world data and case studies.

## 🎯 Key Features

### 1. **Interactive Analytics Dashboard**
- Real-time visualization of 60+ survey responses
- Threat encounter statistics and distribution analysis
- 2FA (Two-Factor Authentication) adoption metrics
- Overall risk assessment indicators
- Dynamic charts using Chart.js (Doughnut & Bar charts)

### 2. **Personalized Risk Analyzer**
- Interactive cyber risk scoring system
- Evaluates 4 key security factors:
  - Password reuse practices
  - 2FA implementation
  - Password update frequency
  - Suspicious link clicking behavior
- Real-time radar chart visualization
- Risk categorization: Low 🟢 | Medium 🟡 | High 🔴

### 3. **Legal Framework Guide**
Comprehensive coverage of Indian cyber laws including:
- **IT Act 2000 Sections:**
  - Section 43: Unauthorized access & data damage (₹1 Crore penalty)
  - Section 66: Hacking with criminal intent (3 years + ₹5 Lakhs)
  - Section 66C: Identity theft (3 years + ₹1 Lakh)
  - Section 66D: Cheating by impersonation (3 years + ₹1 Lakh)
  - Section 66E: Privacy violation (3 years + ₹2 Lakhs)
  - Section 66F: Cyber terrorism (Life imprisonment)
  - Section 67: Obscene content (3-5 years + ₹5-10 Lakhs)
  - Section 72: Breach of confidentiality (2 years + ₹1 Lakh)

- **Related IPC Sections:**
  - IPC 419: Cheating by impersonation
  - IPC 420: Cheating & dishonesty
  - IPC 463, 465, 468, 471: Forgery-related offenses

### 4. **Real-World Case Studies**
Four detailed cybercrime investigations from India (2024-2025):

#### Case 1: AI Voice Deepfake Scam - Mumbai (2025)
- **Victim:** Business owner, 58 years
- **Loss:** ₹4.2 Lakhs
- **Method:** AI voice cloning from social media, emergency impersonation
- **Prevention:** Family safe words, video verification

#### Case 2: WhatsApp Business Account Takeover - Delhi (2025)
- **Victim:** Small textile business
- **Loss:** ₹12.8 Lakhs + reputation damage
- **Method:** SIM swap attack, customer fraud
- **Prevention:** Email-based 2FA, SIM swap protection

#### Case 3: Cryptocurrency Investment Scam - Bangalore (2024-2025)
- **Victims:** 2,300+ investors nationwide
- **Loss:** ₹86 Crores total
- **Method:** Ponzi scheme, fake trading platform, celebrity deepfakes
- **Outcome:** 7 arrests, ₹18 crores recovered

#### Case 4: Ransomware Attack on Hospital - Pune (2025)
- **Target:** 250-bed hospital facility
- **Ransom:** $50,000 in Bitcoin
- **Impact:** 72 hours downtime, 1,200+ patient records encrypted
- **Success:** Restored from backups, no ransom paid

## 🛡️ Security Recommendations

### For Individuals:
- ✅ Enable 2FA on all accounts
- ✅ Use unique passwords (12+ characters)
- ✅ Use password managers (Bitwarden, 1Password)
- ✅ Verify emergency calls via video
- ✅ Limit social media voice/video sharing
- ✅ Never share OTPs or passwords

### For Businesses:
- ✅ Implement zero-trust architecture
- ✅ Regular security awareness training
- ✅ Offline backup strategy (3-2-1 rule)
- ✅ Incident response plan and drills
- ✅ Network segmentation
- ✅ Email-based 2FA for business accounts

## 🚨 Report Cybercrime

### Golden Hour Rule
Report within **1 hour** for maximum fund recovery:
- **Within 1 hour:** 70% recovery rate
- **Within 24 hours:** 40% recovery rate
- **After 48 hours:** <10% recovery rate

### Contact Information:
- **National Helpline:** 1930 (24/7 toll-free)
