Case Study: AI Voice Scam in India

The Incident

In recent months, multiple reports across Indian metros have highlighted a sophisticated "Deepfake Audio" scam. Cybercriminals use AI-generated voice cloning to impersonate relatives in distress to extort money.

How it Works

  • Data Collection: Scammers harvest short audio clips from social media videos.
  • AI Cloning: Using AI tools, they replicate the victim's voice with 95% accuracy.
  • The Hook: A frantic call is made to a family member claiming an emergency (accident/arrest).

Key Takeaways

  • Verification is critical before any financial transfer.
  • Multi-factor authentication (2FA) prevents account takeovers used to find contacts.
  • Public awareness of AI capabilities is the first line of defense.

Expert Recommendations

Always establish a "Family Password" or a secret phrase to verify identity during emergency calls. Never trust the Caller ID alone, as numbers can be spoofed.
